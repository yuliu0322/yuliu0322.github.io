Summary: >
  This study optimized the preparation of transglutaminase-crosslinked
  lactoferrin particles and evaluated their use as stabilizers for
  oleogel-based Pickering emulsions. The particles exhibited suitable
  wettability for emulsion stabilization and were combined with oleogels
  structured using different combinations of carnauba wax and behenyl
  alcohol. In vitro digestion showed that the carnauba-wax oleogel system
  produced the highest lipid digestion rate and curcumin bioaccessibility,
  demonstrating the potential of these emulsions as food-grade delivery
  systems for lipophilic bioactive compounds.
