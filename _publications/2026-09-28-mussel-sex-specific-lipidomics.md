summary: >
  This study used UPLC-HRMS-based lipidomics to compare the gonadal lipid
  profiles of male and female thick-shell mussels (Mytilus coruscus).
  A total of 140 differential lipids were identified, revealing distinct
  sex-dependent patterns in lipid composition and metabolism. Females
  showed higher levels of glycerophospholipids enriched with polyunsaturated
  fatty acids, whereas males contained more glycerolipids, particularly
  triacylglycerols enriched with saturated fatty acids. These findings
  highlight differences in membrane lipid remodeling and energy storage
  between sexes, providing insights into mussel reproductive physiology,
  nutritional evaluation, and the targeted utilization of marine resources.
