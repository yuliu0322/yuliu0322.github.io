summary: >
  This review examines microplastic contamination in oysters from the
  perspectives of detection, biological effects, food safety, and mitigation.
  It compares commonly used extraction, identification, and quantification
  methods; summarizes the occurrence and accumulation of microplastics in
  oysters; and discusses their potential effects on oyster physiology and
  human health. The review also evaluates depuration as a strategy for reducing
  microplastic loads and highlights the need for standardized analytical
  procedures, realistic exposure studies, and optimized depuration conditions
  to support reliable risk assessment and safer oyster consumption.
