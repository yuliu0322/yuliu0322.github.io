I'm happy to share that our previous research on the sex-specific lipid profiles of the marine mussel *Mytilus coruscus* has been published in Food Chemistry. In this study, we compared the lipid composition and metabolism of male and female mussels to better understand sex-related differences in their nutritional and biochemical characteristics.

It is great to see this work officially published, and I'm very grateful to everyone who contributed to the study. This research was part of our earlier work on seafood quality and biochemical characterization, and it is exciting to see it now reach publication.
