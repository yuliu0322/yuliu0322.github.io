Building on the work I presented at ISSCS 2026, I shared our latest results on bioinspired self-cooling nanocomposites made from ChNF and gelatin. The poster highlighted new findings on ChNF structure and charge, mechanical properties, and the materials’ combined passive radiative and evaporative cooling performance. I was pleasantly surprised by the level of interest: the poster sparked many conversations about how these materials work and where they could be used. I spent much of the session discussing the results with attendees—and nearly lost my voice by the end!
